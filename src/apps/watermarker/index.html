Watermarker

A watermark is a logo, signature, or stamp placed over a photo or document to mark it as yours before sharing a preview or proof. Watermarker lets you place one — or a whole grid of them — over a batch of images or every page of a PDF at once.

Everything happens right here in your browser — your files are never uploaded anywhere.
