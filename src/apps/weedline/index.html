Weedline

A weeding boundary is an extra cut line drawn around a design so the excess vinyl or HTV peels away in one clean piece instead of bit by bit. Weedline traces your design's outline and draws that boundary for you.

Everything happens right here in your browser — your image is never uploaded anywhere.
