Rhinestone Fill Creator

Set the size of your piece, drop a photo underneath as a guide, pick your stone sizes and colors, then fill the area with a grid or radial pattern and add or remove stones by hand. Export true-size SVG files with a circle for every stone.

Everything happens right here in your browser — your image never leaves your device.

Tip: pick the “Move image” tool above the canvas and drag to reposition.

3. Stone sizes

Tap every ss size you're working with. Sizes are nominal diameters; brands vary slightly.

4. Stone colors

Select as many as you like. The most recently tapped is the active color.

5. Fill

Pick a second size to alternate stone by stone, switching on every row like a checkerboard.

Fills the whole working area with the fill size(s) and the active color (or all selected colors if rotating). It ignores the backing image.

6. Export SVG

Files are exactly your working-area size (width and height in inches), with each circle at its true stone diameter. Per-size exports are zipped, and every file shares the same area so they line up when stacked.
